A letter for Rik

How I became your girl

Hey baby,

Firstly, Happy Birthday, my love! May all your wishes come true—though I’d say one of your biggest wishes already came true, because you got me! 😉

You are such a good, humble, and genuine person, and I am so incredibly lucky to have you in my life. I know things have been going up and down lately, and our emotions have been tested, but I deeply appreciate the way you hold onto us and ground our relationship.

I know I sometimes say you don’t love me, but deep down, I know you love me so much. I only say it because I get scared—scared that someone’s nazar might touch us, because I truly cannot afford to lose you. You are my life. I know what you’ll say when you hear this, but you can’t change the truth!

Right from the very beginning, I felt this deep connection to you. From day one, I could see our entire future together. I know things are tough right now and we’re going through a rough patch in life, but I know we will get through it. I am all yours, forever.

Rik, whenever I fight with you or get upset, it’s only because I miss you and want your time. I don’t care about expensive gifts—your time is priceless to me. Thank you for standing by me and supporting me through every phase of my life.

I am so, so proud of you. You are handling everything with so much grace and strength, and I see how hard you work. Just don’t give up. I promise to stand by your side and support you always and forever.

I love you so, so much, baby. Happy Birthday! ❤️

— yours, always
